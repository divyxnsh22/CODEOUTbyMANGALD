How this works

  1. 01
    Write or drop in your code. Everything happens in this browser tab — nothing is uploaded or saved anywhere as you type.
  2. 02
    Pick a way out. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera, email it to yourself, or just download it.
  3. 03
    Close the tab. Nothing was ever stored on this computer, so there's nothing left to clean up.

Nothing here touches a server this site doesn't need. Your code never leaves your browser except in the exact moment you choose to email it or scan it — there's no database, no login, and no history saved on this machine.

Built for students on shared lab computers. Fork it on GitHub and host your own copy for free with GitHub Pages.
